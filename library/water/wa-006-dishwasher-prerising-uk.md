> Main findings

• Pre-rinsing dishes before loading a dishwasher adds water consumption that is not included in the dishwasher's own cycle consumption.
• In the UK, approximately 9% of the total number of dishes cleaned in a dishwasher had been pre-rinsed.
• UK pre-rinsing was estimated to use approximately 4.4 L of water per dishwasher cycle.
• The corresponding figure reported for Italy was 19.7 L per dishwasher cycle.
• A 2016 UK consumer behaviour study found that 32% of consumers normally pre-rinsed dishes under the tap.
