> Main findings

• The review summarizes scientific research on manual and electric dishwashing.
• Manual dishwashing water consumption varied substantially between participants and countries.
• In a study involving 12 place settings, manual dishwashing water consumption ranged from 34.7 L to 160.1 L.
• Participants washing dishes under running tap water had high water consumption.
• The lowest manual water consumption was achieved when participants used a filled sink.
• Dishwasher water consumption in the reviewed studies ranged from 12.0 L to 17.7 L for the compared loads.
• In one comparison, manual dishwashing used 103 L while the dishwasher used 15 L for 12 place settings.
