> Main findings

• Average water consumption of dishwashers in the EU was approximately 10 L per cycle in 2020.
• More efficient dishwashers can use approximately 9 L of water for a load of up to 15 place settings.
• Dishwasher water consumption has decreased substantially compared with older generations of appliances.
