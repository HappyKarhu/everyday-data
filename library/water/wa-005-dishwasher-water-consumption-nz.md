> Main findings

• Consumer NZ testing found that the average dishwasher used approximately 13 L of water to clean a full load.
• The test kitchen sink held 13 L when filled to two-thirds capacity.
• Washing the same quantity of dishes by hand required at least three sink-loads, or approximately 39 L.
• The hand-washing result included pre-rinsing.
