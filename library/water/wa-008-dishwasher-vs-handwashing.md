> Main findings

• Which? tested 33 recently tested dishwashers and compared their water consumption with an estimated handwashing scenario.

• The estimated handwashing consumption was 4.5 L per place setting, based on using a 9-L washing-up bowl for approximately two place settings.

• Dishwasher main programmes used an average of 0.926 L per place setting.

• Dishwasher eco programmes used an average of 0.696 L per place setting.

• For 14 place settings, the estimated water consumption was approximately 63.0 L for handwashing, 12.96 L for a dishwasher main programme, and 9.74 L for a dishwasher eco programme.

• The comparison shows that dishwasher water consumption can be substantially lower than the estimated handwashing scenario.

> Important methodological note

The dishwasher values come from Which? practical testing.

The handwashing value is an estimated comparison scenario rather than a direct measurement of typical household handwashing behaviour. It is based on a 9-L washing-up bowl and approximately two place settings per bowl.

Therefore, the handwashing figure should be treated as a modelled estimate rather than a universal average.
