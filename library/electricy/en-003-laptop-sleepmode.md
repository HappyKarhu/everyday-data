Main findings

• ENERGY STAR Version 9.0 evaluates computers in several operating states, including Off, Sleep and Idle.

• The specification applies to different computer types, including notebooks, and uses product-specific requirements and power-management criteria.

• The ENERGY STAR Certified Computers database provides model-specific electricity values for Off Mode, Sleep Mode, Long Idle and Short Idle.

• Power consumption varies between individual notebook models; therefore, a single generic standby value should not be assigned to all laptops.

• The distinction between Sleep and Off is directly relevant when evaluating the electricity consumption of leaving a laptop in Sleep mode overnight.

Important methodological note

The ENERGY STAR database provides model-specific certified product data. The values in the Everyday Data dataset therefore represent the selected certified notebook models and should not be described as the average electricity consumption of all laptops.

The models differ in hardware configuration, power-management implementation and low-power technology. These differences may affect Sleep and Idle electricity consumption.

The dataset is being used to compare current notebook models and later calculate the electricity associated with leaving a laptop in Sleep mode versus shutting it down.
